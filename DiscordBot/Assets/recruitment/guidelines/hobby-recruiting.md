# ⚠️ HOBBY — TEAMS RECRUITING DEVS

Use this forum to find **unpaid helpers or collaborators** for your project.

## Your Post Must Include:
* **Project Pitch:** What is your game about? (Include screenshots/concept if possible)
* **Roles Needed:** (e.g., Looking for 1 Pixel Artist)
* **Your Role:** What are *you* contributing to the project?
* **Expectations:** Scope of the game, deadlines, and expected hours.
* **Compensation:** Strictly unpaid or Rev-Share terms.

*Note: Revenue share is NOT guaranteed pay. If you are offering upfront or guaranteed milestones, use the Paid forum.*

## 🔒 Safety & Rules
* **Keep info public:** Discuss scope and project details in the thread. Do not hide team structure in DMs.
* **DM Safety:** Move to DMs only for private info. Never run unknown files or share passwords.
* **Active Listings:** Max 1 active listing. Close your old post before making a new one.
