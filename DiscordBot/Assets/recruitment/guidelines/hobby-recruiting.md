# Hobby recruiting

State the **project and progress**, **roles/skills needed**, **tasks**, **time commitment**, **contact**, and **unpaid or revenue-share terms**. Show existing work where possible. Do not portray potential revenue as guaranteed pay.

Read the pinned rules; use accurate tags. Verify claims and agree terms in writing.

**One open listing across all four recruitment forums.** Combine hiring and service offers in a for-hire listing.

Enter **{{code}}** via **Acknowledge** within **{{acknowledgementMinutes}} minutes** of the bot prompt. Unacknowledged posts are removed. Listing/cooldown conflicts close promptly.

Accepted listings start a **{{cooldownDays}}-day cooldown** from creation. Closing/deleting won't reset it. Repeated attempts trigger a temporary pause; check **/recruitment eligibility**.

Keep this listing updated or bump it when still looking; its history stays visible. Mark finished listings **Closed** to lock/archive. Auto-archive does not free your slot.
