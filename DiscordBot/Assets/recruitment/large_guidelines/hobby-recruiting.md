# ⚠️ HOBBY — TEAMS / RECRUITING

Use this forum to recruit collaborators for **unpaid hobby or revenue-share projects**.

## Your post must include

* **Project name / goal**
* **Role(s)** you need
* **Current team** and project status
* **Scope / responsibilities**
* Expected **time commitment**
* **Compensation:** Unpaid or Rev-Share
* Expectations around **credit, ownership and usage**
* How applicants will be **assessed or selected**

**Revenue share is not guaranteed pay** and must be labelled clearly. If you are offering guaranteed payment, use Paid instead.

Link your **website, itch.io/Steam page, GitHub, previous projects, prototypes or other relevant work** where available. Give people enough information to understand what they are joining.

## Keep useful questions public

Ask about **scope, expectations, workload, ownership, revenue share and project details in the thread** when the answer would benefit everyone.

Do not immediately push applicants into DMs. Bad actors prefer private conversations because there is less visibility, making suspicious behaviour harder for users and moderators to identify.

Move to DMs when applications or genuinely private information need to be exchanged.

**Never ask collaborators to run unknown files, share credentials, make purchases, or send money to join your project.**
