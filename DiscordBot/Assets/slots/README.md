# Slot machine art

`cherry.png`, `lemon.png`, `grape.png`, `bell.png`, `star.png` and `diamond.png` are the 3D variants of the Cherries, Lemon, Grapes, Bell, Star and Gem stone emoji from [Microsoft Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), used under the MIT license in `LICENSE-FluentEmoji.txt`.

The red 7 is drawn at render time from `Assets/fonts/ConsolasBold.ttf` by `SlotsRenderer`.
